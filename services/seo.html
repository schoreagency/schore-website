WHAT SEO ACTUALLY MEANS
Search engine optimisation (SEO) is the practice of making your website appear in Google and Bing search results for the keywords your customers are searching. For e-commerce brands, this means ranking for product-related searches (buying intent) and content searches (research intent). For SaaS and B2B, this means ranking for problem-aware and solution-aware queries. SEO is the only marketing channel that compounds: the content you publish today keeps driving traffic in 3 years. The backlinks you earn this quarter strengthen every future page you publish. Done correctly, SEO becomes your cheapest customer acquisition channel by year two.
