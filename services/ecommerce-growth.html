// COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

What does a full-stack retainer include?
It varies by scope, but typically includes: paid media management (Meta + Google), content strategy and production, email flow management, SEO execution, and monthly strategy reviews.
What revenue stage do you work with?
We work best with brands doing $30K–$500K/month in revenue looking to scale. Below $30K/month, the retainer cost may not be justified. Above $500K, we discuss custom partnerships.
How long before we see meaningful growth?
Quick wins are visible in weeks (CRO, email flows). Paid media compounds over 2-3 months. SEO compounds over 6-12 months. Sustainable growth requires a 12-month commitment.
Do you work on retainer or project basis?
Primarily retainer — growth is a continuous process, not a project. We do take on focused project work for specific deliverables like brand builds, website redesigns, or audit engagements.
