WHAT PERFORMANCE MARKETING ACTUALLY MEANS
Performance marketing is paid media that is held accountable to measurable business outcomes — revenue, ROAS, CAC, signups, demos. Unlike brand advertising, performance marketing is direct-response: you spend, you measure, you optimize, you scale. It covers Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Display, YouTube), TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and Pinterest Ads. Done well, performance marketing compounds — the more data you feed it, the cheaper and more effective it becomes.

// COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

What platforms do you manage?
Primarily Meta (Facebook + Instagram), Google (Search, Shopping, YouTube), and TikTok. We also manage LinkedIn for B2B clients and Pinterest for lifestyle/beauty brands.
What budget do you require to get started?
We recommend a minimum ad spend of $3,000/month to have enough data to optimize effectively. Most of our clients are spending $10,000–$100,000/month in managed spend.
How long before we see results?
The first 2-4 weeks are typically a learning phase. Significant performance improvements are usually visible by month 2, with ROAS compounding through month 3-6 as the algorithm learns and creative improves.
Do you produce creative or do we need to supply it?
Both options are available. We recommend our AI creative system which produces 40+ creatives per month at a fraction of traditional production cost — and our data shows AI-directed creative consistently outperforms studio shoots.
