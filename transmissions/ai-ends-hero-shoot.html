// TRANSMISSION — AI & MARKETING

hero shoot demise

The traditional hero shoot, once a staple of e-commerce advertising, is no longer economically viable. AI has changed the ad production landscape, rendering this approach obsolete.

2026-06-29 // SCHORE

the traditional model

For years, brands have relied on high-end photo shoots to produce a small set of polished, attention-grabbing images. These hero shots were then used across various marketing channels, from social media to email campaigns. However, this approach is costly and time-consuming, with prices ranging from $5,000 to $50,000 or more per shoot, depending on the scope and complexity.

The end result is often a limited set of images that may not resonate with the target audience, and may not be versatile enough for use across different marketing channels.

ai-driven production

AI has introduced a new paradigm in ad production, enabling brands to generate high-quality images at a fraction of the cost. With AI, brands can produce hundreds of images from a single concept, allowing for greater flexibility and customization. This shift has significant implications for e-commerce and DTC brands, which can now allocate their budgets more efficiently.

For instance, a brand could use AI to generate product images showcasing different colors, styles, or scenarios, without the need for multiple photo shoots. This approach can help increase conversion rates and improve customer engagement.

new economics

The adoption of AI in ad production has disrupted the traditional economics of the hero shoot. Brands can now achieve better results with lower investments, freeing up resources for other critical marketing activities. By leveraging AI, brands can optimize their ad spend, improve return on investment, and enhance their overall marketing performance.

As the use of AI in ad production becomes more widespread, we can expect to see a significant reduction in the number of traditional hero shoots. Instead, brands will focus on creating high-quality, AI-generated images that resonate with their target audience and drive meaningful results.
