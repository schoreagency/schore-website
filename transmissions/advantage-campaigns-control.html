algorithm control

As e-commerce and DTC brands scale their Meta Ads campaigns, the question of when to hand control to the algorithm and when to maintain human oversight becomes increasingly important. The balance between algorithmic efficiency and human judgment can significantly impact campaign performance.

2026-06-15 // SCHORE

understanding algorithmic control

Meta Ads' Advantage+ campaigns are designed to automate ad optimization, using machine learning to allocate budget and target high-performing audiences. This can be beneficial for brands with large, complex campaigns, as it allows for more efficient ad spend and reduced manual labor.

However, handing over full control to the algorithm can be risky, particularly for brands with nuanced targeting requirements or sensitive brand messaging. In such cases, human oversight is necessary to ensure that the algorithm is aligned with the brand's goals and values.

when to hand control to the algorithm

Typically, brands can safely hand control to the algorithm when they have a solid understanding of their target audience and a clear, straightforward campaign objective. For example, if a brand is running a straightforward conversion-driven campaign with a well-defined target audience, the algorithm can handle the optimization of ad spend and targeting.

In contrast, if a brand is running a campaign with complex, multi-layered targeting requirements or a highly nuanced brand message, human oversight is necessary to ensure that the algorithm is interpreting the brand's goals correctly.

striking a balance between human and algorithmic control

Ultimately, the key to successful Advantage+ campaigns is striking a balance between human and algorithmic control. By setting clear campaign objectives, defining nuanced targeting requirements, and providing the algorithm with high-quality data, brands can create an environment in which the algorithm can thrive.

At the same time, human marketers must remain vigilant, monitoring campaign performance and adjusting the algorithm's parameters as needed to ensure that the campaign remains aligned with the brand's overall goals and values.
