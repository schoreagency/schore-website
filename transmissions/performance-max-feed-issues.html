Feed Fumbles

Despite its potential, many brands struggle to optimize Performance Max due to a crucial oversight: feed quality. This seemingly minor detail can significantly hinder campaign performance.

2026-07-06 // SCHORE

the importance of feed quality

Feed quality is often viewed as a mundane aspect of campaign setup, but it plays a critical role in determining the success of Performance Max campaigns. A well-structured feed provides Google's algorithms with the necessary information to effectively target and convert users.

Typical e-commerce brands have feeds with thousands of products, each with its own set of attributes and descriptions. Ensuring that these feeds are accurate, up-to-date, and properly formatted is a daunting task, but one that is essential for maximizing campaign performance.

common feed quality issues

One common issue with feed quality is inconsistency in product attributes. For example, using different formatting for product sizes or colors can confuse Google's algorithms and lead to poor targeting.

Another issue is outdated or missing product information, which can result in irrelevant ads being served to users. Illustrative numbers suggest that a typical e-commerce brand may have around 10-20% of their products with missing or outdated information, which can significantly impact campaign performance.

optimizing feed quality for performance max

To optimize feed quality for Performance Max, brands should focus on standardizing product attributes and ensuring that all product information is up-to-date and accurate.

This can be achieved through regular feed audits and the implementation of automated feed management tools. By prioritizing feed quality, brands can provide Google's algorithms with the necessary information to effectively target and convert users, leading to improved campaign performance and increased return on ad spend.
