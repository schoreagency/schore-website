// THE CHALLENGE
WHERE THEY WERE
Primal was spending $40K/month on Meta ads with a 2.1X ROAS — barely profitable. Creative was stale, audiences were burned, and the brand had no differentiation in the most competitive supplement category on the internet.

HOW WE THOUGHT ABOUT IT
Supplements are won on creative, not targeting. We rebuilt the entire creative strategy around education-first content: teach the science, earn the trust, convert with confidence.
