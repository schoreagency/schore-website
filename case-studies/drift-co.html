// 01 — THE BRIEF
// THE CHALLENGE
WHERE THEY WERE
Drift Co had a loyal local following but couldn't crack scale. Strong aesthetic identity but no performance infrastructure. Organic reach declining. Paid ads launched once with no strategy — $12K spent, $400 returned.
// THE APPROACH
HOW WE THOUGHT ABOUT IT
The community was the asset. We built the performance infrastructure around what already worked — the aesthetic, the culture, the audience. Performance follows brand, not the other way around.

// 03 — HOW IT HAPPENED

THE TIMELINE

MONTH 01
INFRASTRUCTURE BUILD
GA4, Pixel, CAPI set up. Meta and TikTok accounts structured. Email flows built. First drop campaign brief created.
MONTH 02
FIRST DROP LAUNCH
Drop launch campaign generated $180K in 72 hours. TikTok organic video hit 2.1M views. Instagram +12K followers.
MONTH 03–04
SCALE THE MODEL
Second and third drops launched using refined playbook. Email list grew to 28K subscribers.
MONTH 05–06
$800K REVENUE
$800K total revenue. 9.1X peak ROAS. 280% Instagram growth. Ambassador program with 40 active creators.
