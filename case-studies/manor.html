// THE CHALLENGE
WHERE THEY WERE
MANOR had been operating for 3 years with stagnant revenue at $80K/month. Despite a quality product, the brand was invisible in a crowded fashion market — no clear identity, generic messaging, and ad spend generating diminishing returns with every passing month.
// THE APPROACH
HOW WE THOUGHT ABOUT IT
Product was strong. Brand was weak. Every competitor looked identical. We built an identity so distinct that ads stopped competing on price and started winning on perception. Brand first — always. Ads follow brand, not the other way around.
