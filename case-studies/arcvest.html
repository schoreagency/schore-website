// THE CHALLENGE
WHERE THEY WERE
Arcvest entered a crowded fintech market with a strong product but zero brand equity, zero audience, and no acquisition strategy. Every competitor had more funding and more brand recognition.
// THE APPROACH
HOW WE THOUGHT ABOUT IT
Brand first. Fintech is a trust industry. You give us your money. Trust is earned through brand credibility, not performance ads. We refused to run a single paid ad until the brand was ready.
